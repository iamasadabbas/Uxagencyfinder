## Information only

UX Agency Finder publishes editorial information about UX design agencies. It's general information to help you research, not professional, legal or procurement advice. Always do your own checks before hiring an agency.

## No endorsement or guarantee

Inclusion or position in a list is an editorial judgement, not a guarantee of an agency's quality, availability, pricing or results. Agency case-study claims are summarised from the agencies' own public pages and haven't been independently audited.

## Your dealings with agencies

Any agreement you make is between you and the agency. We aren't a party to it and we don't receive your details.

## Third-party websites

We link to agency websites we don't control and aren't responsible for.

We may update these terms. The current version is always on this page.
