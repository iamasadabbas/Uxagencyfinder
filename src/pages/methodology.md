## What this site is

UX Agency Finder is an editorial selection of UX design agencies, organised by industry and technology market. It isn't a review platform, a lead-generation service or a paid directory.

## The eight niches

Healthcare, Fintech, Ecommerce, Logistics, Real Estate, Education, Robotics, and AI & ML. The first six are industries. Robotics and AI & ML are technology markets chosen because the products and user tasks in them are distinct.

## Inclusion rules

An agency is included in a niche when a **public source names a relevant project and describes the agency's UX or product contribution** in that niche.

These do **not** count as evidence on their own:

- Visual style, or generic "SaaS" or "enterprise" experience
- A client logo with no described scope
- A marketing website for a company in that industry
- A generic service page claiming industry expertise

Capabilities such as dashboard design, design systems or AI interfaces may appear on profiles as supporting facts, but they never add an agency to an industry list by themselves.

### Documented exception

**CorLab Tech** is included at Logistics position 5 at the site owner's direction. Its public evidence is service-level (a data-visualisation service page and a logistics industry page). We haven't seen a named logistics client case. This is shown wherever the placement appears.

## How positions are set

Each niche has its **own editorial order**, chosen by the site owner and informed by the recorded public evidence. We consider:

- How relevant the named project and its users are to the niche
- How clearly the agency's contribution is described
- Research, workflow and delivery detail that matters for the niche
- The work stage (concept, prototype, pilot, shipped) and source limitations

**Positions are not scores.** We don't calculate ratings, import review stars or claim measured market leadership. Position 1 isn't automatically right for every brief, so read the scope notes.

## Sources

All current evidence comes from **official agency-published pages**, accessed during research. Accessing a page isn't independent verification: outcomes, adoption figures and compliance claims in case studies are agency-reported. Each source in our lists records what it supports and its limits.

We haven't researched budgets, hourly rates, office locations or availability. Missing public evidence doesn't mean an agency lacks a capability. It means we can't substantiate the claim yet.

## Exclusions

Some agencies are excluded from this site at the owner's discretion. Exclusion isn't a negative finding about an agency.

## Updates and corrections

We plan to check sources and links regularly, and to review each industry list and profile at least every six months or when a material change is reported. Review dates only change after an actual review. To report an error, see the [contact page](/contact/).
