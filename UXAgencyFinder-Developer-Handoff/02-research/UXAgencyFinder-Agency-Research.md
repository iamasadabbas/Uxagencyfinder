## 11. Recommended editorial rankings for all eight niches

### Evaluation method

Normally assess eligibility through a relevant named project and documented agency contribution. Consider the niche's actual user tasks, relevance/breadth, clarity of public evidence, research/interaction detail and stage of delivery. The owner has supplied the exact orders below; these considerations explain fit and limitations rather than calculate positions. CorLab Tech at Logistics #5 is an explicit owner-directed exception with service-level evidence and no named logistics case established by the reviewed pages. There is no fabricated numerical formula.

Missing budget, staffing or availability information does not become a guessed score. Agency-reported outcome percentages and fundraising do not determine position. Publisher relationships and owner-selected ordering must be disclosed accurately.

Each list has a different scope. Healthcare includes clinical, administrative, public-health and explicitly labeled wellness examples. Logistics distinguishes shipper/distribution work from warehouse robots and driver self-service. Robotics separates industrial, consumer, educational and simulation tools.

The following table is the complete owner-selected ordering. Use the same sequence in the comparison table, agency assessments, quick picks and ItemList. A narrower future subniche can justify a different subset/order rather than inheriting these positions automatically.
