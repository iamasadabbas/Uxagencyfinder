# UX Agency Finder: developer acceptance checklist

Extracted from section 20 of master brief version 1.6. Use the complete master brief for implementation context. Items below are requirements, not completed website QA.

### Editorial and data

- [ ] The primary taxonomy uses actual industries plus the explicitly requested Robotics and AI & ML markets; generic product/capability tags remain supporting facts.
- [ ] The eight planned niches, including the owner-requested Robotics and AI & ML categories, have explicit scopes, criteria, route plans, and publication states.
- [ ] The old 36 placements and ten-position homepage ranking are not imported or relabeled.
- [ ] The 21-agency/52-source register remains research input; actual public counts derive from reviewed published records.
- [ ] Every industry placement resolves to relevant evidence with its contribution and work stage recorded. CorLab's owner-directed Logistics exception explicitly records service-level evidence, an unconfirmed project contribution and unknown delivery stage.
- [ ] The 21 selected agency identities and 52 selected niche placements match sections 10-11; Clay and Eleken are absent from all public/seed/related-agency records.
- [ ] The qualified Robotics/Real Estate entries and all other project limits are retained; no unsupported niche is inferred from a capability tag.
- [ ] Visible tables, assessments, quick picks, and ItemList share one sequence.
- [ ] Unknown facts, actual review dates, and material relationships remain accurate.

### Function and technical behavior

- [ ] A visitor can choose a published industry, compare the editorial list, inspect a source/profile, and visit the agency.
- [ ] Core content and links work before JavaScript interaction.
- [ ] Directory search/industry filters, clear/reset, sort, result counts, and mobile/keyboard use work.
- [ ] Draft/backlog routes do not appear as completed coverage.
- [ ] The sitemap agrees with the published manifest, using 22 + P + A only when the full planned fixed set publishes.
- [ ] Canonical redirects, real 404s, metadata, structured data, staging protection, forms, accessibility, and mobile performance are checked.
- [ ] Validation rejects industry placements without eligible evidence or a documented owner-directed exception, duplicate slugs/positions, unpublished dependencies, and sequence mismatches. CorLab's service-level label must survive every public rendering.

Quote design, implementation, editorial research/writing, recurring services, and ongoing maintenance separately. The revised architecture requires additional industry research; do not quote the old five service lists as the same deliverable.

Deliver source/lockfile, schemas/content export, research/source register, asset provenance, staging, route manifest, validation report, event map, editor walkthrough, deployment/rollback instructions, and access handover.

Owner/editor inputs: actual domain control, publisher/reviewer identities, relationships, contact destination, service choices, asset reuse basis, final industry coverage, and any later editorial ordering instructions. Reusable product work can continue while publication copy and factual checks are completed.
