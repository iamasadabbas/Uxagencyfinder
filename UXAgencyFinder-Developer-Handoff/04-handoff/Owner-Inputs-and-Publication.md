# Owner inputs and publication tasks

Derived from master brief version 1.6. Development can proceed from the agreed scope and ordering. Resolve operating details before public release.

| Input/task | Implementation consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Domain/DNS and hosting access | Configure the selected production host, HTTPS and redirects through the owner's chosen services. |
| Actual publisher, editor/reviewer and material agency relationships | Complete About, author pages, methodology, disclosures and review metadata. |
| Contact destination and handling | Configure corrections/suggestions and verify successful delivery. |
| Analytics product and Search Console access | Configure the chosen accounts and verify the event map. |
| Original public assessments and hiring resources | Complete industry/profile content and the four planned guides; the research notes provide inputs. |
| Current commercial facts, if published | Research and record source/date/currency/basis, or retain honest unknown states. |
| Qualified source coverage | Preserve wellness, portfolio-level, simulation, prototype and historical-work labels; seek fuller cases where needed. |
| CorLab Tech Logistics evidence | Keep Logistics #5 and the service-level label. Confirm a comparable named engagement and actual UX contribution before adding project-specific delivery claims. |
| Logos and other assets | Obtain reusable files and record the reuse basis; complete text-only cards are acceptable. |
| Production services and recurring costs | Confirm hosting, CMS/editor, contact handling and other selected services. |
| Final public factual/content checks | Publish only completed reviewed records. Source access alone is not editorial approval. |

No numerical agency review scores, budgets, office details, testimonials, clients, compliance claims or outcomes should be added without the required source and qualification.

The selected industry orders and the inclusion of CorLab Tech have already been specified. This checklist does not request a new approval of those choices.
